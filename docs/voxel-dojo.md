# Voxel Dojo

Open Voxel Dojo in the library, then press **Enter dojo**. The session waits at setup until you start. WASD, ZQSD and arrow movement all work with the usual FPS controls.

Cedar Dojo has a sheltered equipment hall, a tatami melee pad, measured shooting lanes, a cover wall, cherry trees, stone lanterns and a torii backstop. The physical scenery blocks movement and shots where it should.

- **Gun rack:** choose any of the 36 guns in the range panel, then use the nearby rack with E. Test the actual firing mode, sights, magazine and reload.
- **Blade rack:** try knife, sword, katana, axe or paired tonfas. Test left-click cuts, right-click knife thrusts or parries, and hits across small gaps.
- **Supply station:** replenish supplies for real grenade and potion tests.
- **Recovery station:** restore health or take a deliberate training wound, then practice F healing using your carried potion.
- **Range panel:** Tab releases the pointer and opens compact controls. Switch stationary/moving targets, reset the drill or choose equipment; return to the range to capture the mouse again.

Targets use the same physical head/body/leg contacts as combat. Hit feedback and damage readouts come from accepted damage, rather than predicted hits. Defeated targets respawn after a short delay; the dojo has no victory or game-over screen. Normal magazines, bolt cycles and reload windows remain. Unlimited reserve ammunition is limited to this sandbox.

Use the marked wall to compare clear shots with obstructed shots. Expanded blade reach still ends at real cover and retains its finite cut direction. The dojo does not change PvP or wave-survival loadouts.
