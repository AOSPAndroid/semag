# Voxel blade combos

These rules apply to Breach, Royale, Last Stand and practice, including Voxel Dojo. Each primary attack needs a fresh click or attack-key press. Holding attack never repeats a cut.

| Weapon | Chain | Opening damage | Finisher damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knife | 3 hits | 28 | 34 |
| Sword | 3 hits | 100 | 120 |
| Katana | 3 hits | 75 | 90 |
| Axe | 2 hits | 140 | 161 |
| Twin tonfas | 4 hits | 42 | 50 |

Damage above is the base value. Last Stand keeps its early-wave melee bonus. Every link must actually damage an enemy; misses and parries do not confirm it. Release and press again in the final 83 milliseconds of recovery to queue the next cut, or within 350 milliseconds after recovery ends. The weapon status displays the chain count, queued input and remaining follow-up window.

Cuts alternate direction, and the final strike has a brighter gold trail, extra damage and 25% stronger push. Contacts can briefly interrupt enemy movement and new attacks. Human hit stun lasts about 83 milliseconds, or 133 milliseconds on a finisher, with a 600-millisecond immunity window. Monsters retain their own stagger resistance and immunity; brutes and later waves are harder to interrupt. Solid walls and living allies still stop the slash and knockback.

A miss, expired window, parry, equipment change, death or controls release clears continuation. Pausing keeps the direction and geometry of a cut already committed. Right-click knife thrusts and timed blade parries keep their existing roles and do not advance a primary combo.

In the Dojo, select the katana and strike a fresh target to test the full 75 → 75 → 90 chain. Targets respawn, so you can practice spacing, return cuts and finishers repeatedly.
