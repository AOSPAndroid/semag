01 / FIND YOUR RANGE

Quick strikes recover faster. Heavy strikes reach farther but leave you open. Bait a miss, then punish the recovery.

02 / MAKE THE READ

Tap block just before a hit for a parry. Hold it to guard. Guarding only works while grounded and facing the attack; heavy hits wear down your stamina.

03 / KEEP THE PRESSURE

Cancel a connected quick strike into a heavy. Jump to change your approach. Dash through a strike during its evasion window, then counter.

Also: arrow keys to move and jump, W to jump, Shift to dash, U to block. Stamina recovers when you stop attacking and guarding. Keyboard recommended.
