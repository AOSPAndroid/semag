    Lockdown

    ROOM—

    Capture and hold the three control points. Every five seconds your team scores for the points it holds: first to 500 wins. Bots fill every empty seat. Your base heals you, and it is where you change class and equipment mid-match. Every knockout drops a heart.

    Equipment

    Start in a T-shirt and shorts, then mix a helmet, armor, pants and shoes. Every piece trades something: protection adds up (capped at 60%), speed and attack bonuses multiply.

    Gun, sword and blast percentages reduce incoming damage. Speed is a change from your class's normal movement speed.

    Weapons

    Four free slots: any weapon in any slot (four swords is fine), or leave one empty to carry hearts. Every weapon has a charge attack: hold the button, release when the ring is full. Right click is your class ability: Striker Boost (hold to sprint while the meter lasts), Guardian Shield (hold; grows into a healing dome), Recon Cloak. Space dashes. Press I in a match to reorder your slots.
