    NO SQUAD TO FALL BACK ON

    Make your one life count.

    The last player alive wins. Everyone spawns at a random, separate position with a sword. Search cabins, courtyards and hidden structures for weapons and supplies. A shrinking storm draws the survivors together.

    01 / SCAVENGE

    Approach a pickup and press E. You carry one gun plus your sword. Picking up a different gun exchanges your current gun. Ammunition, healing potions and grenades are finite supplies: plan your route through cover.

    02 / MOVE & AIM

    WASD or arrows move. SPACE jumps, CTRL crouches and SHIFT walks. Mouse looks, LMB shoots or swings, RMB aims. Settle before firing: movement, jumps and recoil reduce accuracy. Climb crates and ledges to change your angle.

    03 / USE YOUR INVENTORY

    V swaps gun and sword. R reloads. Q throws a frag grenade. F drinks a potion for up to 40 health after two seconds. Damage or another action interrupts healing and spends the potion. Semi-automatic guns, shotgun and crossbow require a fresh click for each shot.

    04 / READ THE STORM

    The map shows your position and the current safe circle. The dashed circle marks the next zone. Outside the safe zone you lose health; later stages hurt more. Leave time to move between cover. Eliminated players can watch the remaining survivors.

    05 / HOST THE BATTLE

    Invite up to ten players using the room code or hostname link. Only the room host starts the battle, with at least two players. Players joining after a battle ends can participate in the next one. The host returns everyone to the lobby for a rematch.

    06 / PLAY ANYWHERE

    Enter arena captures your mouse. If capture is unavailable, hold right mouse and drag to look. Touch players use the Move and Look pads and action buttons. Escape, P and Help release all controls. The online battle keeps running.
