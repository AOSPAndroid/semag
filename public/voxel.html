    LEARN THE ANGLES

    Make each round count.

    First team to four rounds wins. Breach carries the device; Hold protects the two sites. Roles switch after round three. Everyone starts each round with 200 health.

    01 / MOVE WITH PURPOSE

    WASD or arrows move. SPACE jumps. Hold CTRL to crouch or SHIFT to walk. Jump from a low crate to the next ledge to reach higher firing positions. Release Jump between jumps. Moving and jumping reduce accuracy; settle before a precise shot. Small circles on the map mark climbing routes; the map also shows sites and living teammates.

    02 / CONTROL YOUR FIRE

    Mouse looks, LMB fires, hold RMB to aim down sights, R reloads. Choose from nine weapons with 1–9 during setup. Pistol, shotgun and bolt weapons fire once per click; the burst rifle fires three shots per click. Release between these attacks. Moving and jumping still reduce accuracy while aiming.

    03 / COMMIT TO THE SITE

    Breach: carry the device to A or B, stand still and hold E for three seconds. Hold: stand beside the planted device and hold E for five seconds. Its fuse lasts 35 seconds.

    04 / BLADE AND SUPPLIES

    V switches between your gun and sword. Click LMB for a committed, short-range strike; wait for recovery before striking again. Q throws your one bouncing frag grenade. F drinks your one potion, restoring up to 60 health after two seconds. Shooting, taking damage or another action interrupts healing and spends the potion. Supplies reset each round. The earlier G grenade and H potion shortcuts also work.

    05 / HOSTNAME FRIENDLY

    Enter arena captures the mouse when available. If your browser blocks capture, hold RMB and drag to look and aim; left click still fires. On touch, use Move and Look pads plus the action buttons. Hold AIM for sights; tap SWORD, FRAG or POTION.

    06 / SPECIALIST WEAPONS

    The bolt sniper rewards settled scoped shots; a miss exposes you during its long bolt cycle. Hold fire through the LMG’s brief wind-up to sustain a lane, then find cover for its longer reload. Crossbow bolts take time to arrive and drop as they fly: lead moving targets, aim above distant ones and reload with R after each bolt.

    Escape and Help release your inputs. Multiplayer rounds keep running. Health shows your current HP and recent damage; low health turns amber. Your squad’s health appears in its roster.
