    LEARN THE ANGLES

    Make each round count.

    First team to four rounds wins. Breach carries the device; Hold protects the two sites. Roles switch after round three.

    01 / MOVE WITH PURPOSE

    WASD or arrows move. SPACE jumps. Hold CTRL to crouch or SHIFT to walk. Moving and jumping reduce accuracy; stop before a precise shot.

    02 / CONTROL YOUR FIRE

    Mouse looks, LMB shoots, R reloads. Choose a carbine, close-range SMG or precise marksman during setup. Ammunition is finite each round.

    03 / COMMIT TO THE SITE

    Breach: carry the device to A or B, stand still and hold E for three seconds. Hold: stand beside the planted device and hold E for five seconds. Its fuse lasts 35 seconds.

    04 / HOSTNAME FRIENDLY

    Enter arena captures the mouse when available. If your browser blocks capture, hold RMB and drag to look; left click still fires. On touch, use Move and Look pads plus the action buttons.

    Escape and Help release your inputs. Multiplayer rounds keep running. Eliminated players spectate their own team. Rematch returns everyone to the lobby; all players must ready up again.
