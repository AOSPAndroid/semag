READ · REACT · COMMIT

Own the next opening.

WASD or arrows move. Mouse or the right touch pad aims the shoulder camera. Space jumps, Shift sprints, Ctrl crouches, C walks quietly.

LMB / J commits a katana cut. Confirm a hit, then click again to chain three cuts. Holding does not swing repeatedly. Aim your slash with the crosshair; cover stops your blade.

RMB / K performs a short, timed directional parry. Face the incoming blade or kunai. A successful parry creates an opening; holding does not block forever.

E dashes in your movement direction; without movement it dashes forward. It uses stamina and has no invulnerability. Q throws a physical kunai; aim at the crosshair and lead moving targets. F cancels an early blade windup with a feint.

P pauses solo play and releases online controls. Escape releases the cursor or exits fullscreen. If mouse capture is unavailable, hold a mouse button and drag to look. Jump, cut, parry, dash and kunai require a fresh press.
