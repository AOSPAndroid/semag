      Monsters announce their attacks. Small Grave Hounds are fast but fragile; their bite growl gives you time to strike or step away. Rift Leapers crouch before committing to a straight lunge: sidestep or put solid cover between you. Interrupt an Ash Screecher’s rising roar with a blade, or eliminate it before it rallies nearby allies. Hunters rush, brutes need space, and armed enemies punish open ground. Kills can drop immediate healing supplies, carried potions or ammunition, and armed monsters sometimes leave their guns. Reach drops and press E to collect them. Your four inventory slots are flexible, and potions stack to two per slot. Slot 1 starts with your chosen blade (katana by default) and slot 2 with your chosen gun. Close combat deals 60%, 40% and 20% extra damage in waves 1–3 and can briefly interrupt attacks. Later enemies resist stagger; repeated strikes cannot keep them stunned. Drop any item with X or its drop arrow; you can carry four weapons. When full, select a gun to replace it with another gun, or a blade to replace it with another blade. Otherwise, drop an item first. F drinks your carried potion; stay still in cover. Clear a wave to recover, resupply and bring fallen teammates back. In co-op, releasing your controls keeps the squad’s battle running.
