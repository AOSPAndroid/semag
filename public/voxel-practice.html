FEEL THE MOVEMENT

Control your next shot.

WASD or arrows move. Mouse looks. Space jumps, Ctrl crouches, Shift sprints, C walks quietly. Release Jump before another jump.

LMB / B / J fires. Hold RMB with a gun for slower, precise sights. With a knife, tap it for a stronger committed stab; with other blades, time it to parry an incoming melee attack. Release before another stab or parry. R reloads. 1–4 equips any of four flexible inventory slots. Mouse wheel cycles guns and blades, skipping supplies and empty slots. X drops the selected item; tap its arrow on touch. V switches gun and blade. Semi-auto, shotgun and bolt weapons need a fresh click.

Q throws a bouncing frag. F drinks a potion for up to 60 health; protect the two-second commitment. Damage and other actions interrupt it.

E picks up nearby loot. Potions stack to two per slot. With a full pack, select a gun to replace it with another gun, or a blade to replace it with another blade. Otherwise, drop an item first. In Breach, start with a knife and your chosen gun; the other two slots are empty. No starting potions or grenades. Press E near fallen enemies to scavenge their gear. In Royale, start with a knife, search structures and stay inside the storm. Bots scavenge the same supplies. The last survivor wins.

Classic: click RMB for up to three simultaneous rounds. Bucky: RMB fires one shell that opens into five pellets after 7.5 m. Release before using either again. Stinger and Bulldog fire committed bursts through their sights. Sidearms and shotguns without sights keep hip-fire look speed.

The blue line below health shows sprint stamina. Sprint forward on the ground; aiming, attacking and other commitments stop sprinting. Release sprint after exhaustion and recover before starting again. Escape pauses solo practice and releases the cursor. If your browser cannot capture it, hold a mouse button and drag to look. Touch: left pad moves, right pad looks, buttons handle actions.
